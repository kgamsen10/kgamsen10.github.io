    PROJECT TERMS

    Payment

    A 50% deposit is required before work begins. The remaining 50% is due upon final approval and before final files are delivered.

    Revisions

    Each package includes the number of revision rounds listed in its description. Additional revisions or major changes in direction are billed separately.

    Project Timeline

    Project timelines begin once the deposit, project details, and required assets have been received. Client delays may affect the final delivery date.

    Client Responsibilities

    Clients are responsible for providing accurate content, images, logos, and other required materials in a timely manner.

    Ownership & Final Files

    Final approved designs are transferred to the client upon full payment. Unused concepts, drafts, and working files remain the property of the designer unless otherwise agreed.

    Portfolio Usage

    Completed projects may be displayed in my portfolio, website, and social media for promotional purposes unless otherwise agreed.

    Project Cancellation

    Deposits are non-refundable once work has begun. If a project is cancelled after work begins, completed work will be billed based on the portion of the project completed.
